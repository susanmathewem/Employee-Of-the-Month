🏆 Employee of the Month

A 60-second office corridor dash — collect wins, dodge chaos!

Choose Your Character

How To Play

Run down the corridor for exactly 60 seconds. Collect positive items, avoid workplace hazards, and grab power-ups to boost your run. Score the highest to become Employee of the Month!

🎮 Controls

  • ↑ / W / Swipe Up — move to upper lane
  • ↓ / S / Swipe Down — move to lower lane
  • Space / → / Tap — jump over a hazard

🧮 Scoring

  • ✅ Each positive collectible: +10 points
  • ⚠️ Each hazard hit: −5 points (jump to dodge!)
  • 📏 Distance travelled adds small bonus points automatically
  • Score can never go below 0, and freezes the instant time runs out

⏱️ Duration

  • You have exactly 60 seconds — timer is always visible
  • Timer flashes at 10 seconds remaining as a warning
  • Game stops automatically at 0 seconds
